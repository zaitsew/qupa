# Roadmap

One screen per phase. Features that ship are in the [README](../README.md).

## v0.1 (this release)

- Phone-as-buzzer trivia over the home network: scan, type a nickname, play.
- Remote-only team mode: 2 to 4 teams take turns on one remote.
- About 5,000 open-licensed questions (5,272 at release), 24 categories.
- English, Spanish and Russian screens.

## v0.2

- Spanish and Russian question packs.
- A "family" filter by difficulty.
- Custom quiz packs typed on a phone.

## v0.3

- More game types on the same phone-controller engine: a drawing guess, "who said
  it", and a Wits-style numeric closest-answer round.

## Later, only on a trigger

- Optional paid question packs through Play Billing. Never ads.

Qupa turns your Google TV into a party quiz show. Everyone plays on their own phone. Nobody installs an app and nobody makes an account.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Open Qupa on the TV and choose "Play with phones".
2. Scan the QR code with a phone camera, or type the address shown.
3. Type a nickname and you are in. Up to 8 players.
4. Questions, the countdown and the scoreboard are on the big screen. Everyone answers on their own phone.

Faster answers score more: a correct answer is worth 500 to 1,000 points. After every question the TV shows the right answer and the scoreboard, and after the last one a podium. Play again keeps the same players.

NO PHONES? PLAY WITH THE REMOTE
Two to four teams take turns on one remote. The team in turn picks an answer with the arrow keys and OK.

YOU CHOOSE THE GAME
- 10, 15 or 20 questions
- 10, 15, 20 or 30 seconds to answer
- Easy, medium, hard or any difficulty
- Pick the categories you like: science, film, music, history, geography, sports, animals and more, 24 in all

MADE FOR THE SOFA
- Dark theme and large type you can read from across the room
- Full control with the remote's D-pad
- Colour and shape on every answer, so nobody relies on colour alone
- Sound effects you can switch off
- Screens in English, Spanish and Russian (the questions are in English for now; Spanish and Russian question packs are planned)

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No ads, no accounts, no tracking. The game runs on your home network: the TV serves the game page to the phones, so nothing goes through the internet. The server runs only while a game is open.

Over 5,000 questions are built in. Qupa prefers ones you have not seen yet.

Qupa is free and open source: github.com/zaitsew/qupa

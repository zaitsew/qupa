# Qupa

**A party quiz for Google TV and Android TV. Phones are the buzzers.**

Open Qupa on the TV, scan the QR code with a phone, type a nickname and play.
Nobody installs anything and nobody signs up. The TV runs the game on your home
network, so the questions, answers and nicknames never go through the internet.

- **Up to 8 players** on their own phones, or **2 to 4 teams** sharing the remote.
- **Fast answers score more.** A correct answer is worth 500 to 1,000 points.
- **Over 5,000 questions** in 24 categories, with difficulty and time you choose.
- **Made for the sofa.** Dark theme, large type, full D-pad control.
- **English, Spanish and Russian** screens (questions are in English for now).
- **No ads. No accounts. No tracking.**
